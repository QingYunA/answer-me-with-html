## Highlights

- **`er` component for entity relationship diagrams** (#140). Write entities with their fields (`name [type] [PK|FK|UK]`) and relationships such as `User 1--* Order: places`. `FK -> Entity` draws the relationship it implies, and each end shows its cardinality (one, zero or many).
- **Change markers in `er`** (#157). `+ `, `- ` and `~ ` now work on entities, fields and relationships, as they do in `flow` and `tree`. One diagram shows what a schema change adds, removes and changes, with the Before / Changes / After switch.
- **Right-to-left pages** (#133). Hebrew, Arabic, Persian, Urdu and Yiddish drafts render right to left. Diagrams mirror (`flow`, `sequence`, `timeline`, `tree`, `limits`), Hebrew gets its own labels, and code, paths and numbers stay left to right.
- **`am serve`** (#146). Open pages from a remote or headless machine over an SSH tunnel. `am serve` listens on `127.0.0.1`, and `render`, `patch` and `video` print a private link for each page.
- **Video player: chapters, speed and export without ffmpeg** (#143). A chapter strip jumps between scenes, a speed button goes from 0.5x to 2x, and `--mp4` writes a WebM made by the page itself when ffmpeg is not installed.
- **Star link in the page footer** (#160). The footer now ends with `★ Star on GitHub`. It is plain text with no badge image, so pages stay offline-safe.
- **Website at https://answer-me-with-html.com** (#128). The old github.io address redirects there.

## Fixes

- **The data directory is private to you** (#156). `~/.answer-me-with-html/` is now created with mode `0700`, and existing installs are tightened. Before, other users on a shared host could read your pages from disk.
- Video narration uses a voice for the language of each line, picked from the voices installed on the machine (#142). A line with no matching voice keeps its captions instead of being read by a voice for another language.
- The video duration estimate counts words in Cyrillic, Arabic, Greek, Hebrew and Thai (#138).
- Flow group names move clear of edges, edge labels and nodes (#148).
- Table words stay whole in narrow sheet panels and in print (#149).
- A horizontal timeline turns vertical on a phone (#135) and in any panel narrower than 560 px (#153).
