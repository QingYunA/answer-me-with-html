## Highlights

- **Math in prose** (#178). `$…$` is inline math, and `$$` on its own line before and after the TeX makes a block. [Temml](https://github.com/ronkok/Temml) typesets it into MathML when the page is made, so the page loads no script, no CDN and no fonts from the network. Bad TeX shows in red with an `L<line> [math]` warning. A Remark or a Reply quotes a formula as the TeX the draft wrote (#185), and the website playground typesets math too (#182).
- **Remark mode** (#136). A Remark button joins the toolbar. Click a heading, paragraph, list item, quote, code block or table, tag it as a suggestion, keep, question or concern, and add a note. The Reply button carries the marks back to the agent in a `## Remarks` section, together with the answers and the panel comments.
- **Share pages with other devices** (#168). `am serve --lan` listens on your LAN IPv4 address, and `--public-url` prints links under a reverse proxy or tunnel origin. Both are opt-in, and `am serve` stays loopback-only by default.
- **New-version notice on the page** (#188, #190). When the weekly check finds a newer version, each page you make has a "New version" button in the toolbar. It opens the update command for your install, with a copy button, and up to three highlights of the new version in the page language. The page itself makes no network request, and `am serve` removes the button from pages it sends to other devices. You'll see it from the release after this one.
- **The colophon line moved to the top of the page** (#186), above the title, where readers see it.
- **Right-to-left videos** (#173). Hebrew and other right-to-left video pages lay out right to left, while the player controls stay left to right. The title block and the scene headers read their labels from the language file (#174).

## Fixes

- A page with STE, code or math warnings no longer opens by itself, and `am render --replace <page>` replaces the earlier attempt once the new page is written, so one answer leaves one page (#166).
- Test fixes for the planner timing guard and the print layout check (#170, #164), and for the math Reply check after the colophon moved (#189).
