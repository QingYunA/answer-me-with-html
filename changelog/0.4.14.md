## Highlights

- **Diff code blocks** (#120). A ` ```diff ` fence now draws a unified diff: red and green lines, old and new line numbers from the `@@` headers, and a `+N −N` stat in the header. `file=`, `title=` or the `+++ b/path` line names the file, and `hl=` uses new-side numbers. A hunk whose counts don't match its `@@` header gets a `[diff-counts]` warning.
- **Diagram text wraps by script** (#112). Korean breaks at its spaces instead of inside a word. Thai, Lao, Khmer and Myanmar, which write no space between words, wrap by dictionary word, so a long label no longer pushes a node wider than its budget. Chinese, Japanese and English diagrams are unchanged. Thanks @SU-AN-coder.

## Fixes

- The writing check counts words in Lao, Khmer and Myanmar sentences with the same word segmenter as Thai. Lao sentences used to miss the length warning, and Khmer and Myanmar sentences got false ones (#117). Thanks @SU-AN-coder.
- `am lint` now follows the draft language, as `am render` and `am video` do. A Thai draft gets the same warnings from both, and a French draft no longer gets the English rules (#119). Thanks @SU-AN-coder.
